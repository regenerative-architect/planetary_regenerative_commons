    Documentation & scientific caution

    How to read—and challenge—the Commons

    Every grouping, label, and score remains inspectable. Exact content hashes answer identity; taxonomy and readiness remain reviewable judgments.

    Deduplication and version collapse
    1. Count ZIP files, never directory entries.
    2. Collapse byte-identical files by SHA-256.
    3. Compare normalized visible HTML after removing markup, scripts, styles, templates, and SVG.
    4. Group probable versions by conservative title/path rules while retaining every public variant, reason, confidence, and hash.
    5. Keep translations and place-specific adaptations distinct when purpose differs.

    Important: hashes prove byte identity or integrity—not authorship, originality, truth, safety, or impact.

    Taxonomy: concepts through deployments
    Two-axis innovation status

    Knowledge status asks whether the underlying claim is established, integrated, hypothesized, or speculative. Build status asks whether the artifact is an experimental prototype or a candidate only for a bounded pilot.

    No item is called field-tested, production, certified, or a breakthrough. Archive timestamps show that bytes existed; they do not validate claims.

    Compatibility, evidence, safety & maturity scores

    Most records use automated documentation signals. A small representative set has curator-reviewed rubric scores. Neither is a probability, certification, or substitute for domain review.

    Privacy and security quarantine

    Potential credentials, private-key blocks, personal conversation/media exports, logs, and repository internals are excluded from public records. The Commons never embeds raw corpus text or files. Incident, medical, legal, geopolitical, biosafety, and dual-use material should receive additional review before any wider publication.

    Scientific caution and safe progression

    Self-descriptions such as “research-grade,” “production-grade,” “safe,” “ISO compliant,” “prototype_ready,” or “breakthrough” are not repeated as verified facts.

    A responsible progression is: speculative concept → testable hypothesis → controlled prototype → ethics-reviewed field-test candidate → independently documented field result. Evidence—not author declaration—moves an item forward.

    Local-first persistence and portability

    Favorites, mixer selections, and private notes stay in IndexedDB on this device, with a localStorage fallback. Export creates a portable workspace JSON; import validates IDs and limits note sizes. Reset removes only this page’s local workspace.

    Four mandatory gates

    • Evidence: What would falsify the claim?
    • Safety: Who can be harmed or excluded?
    • Consent: Who owns the decision and data?
    • Rights: What license and attribution apply?
